about=Processing tool "Classify 1878–80 map (YOLO + rules)". A YOLO26 model finds tufts and peat cuttings (Torfstich) on the 1 m map; dashes are measured per field; the rules of the official 1898 key (Zeichen-Erklärung für die Messtischblätter) turn them into the classes Moor, nasse Wiese and Heide on a 25 m grid. Developed and checked on the Treene–Sorge lowland in Schleswig-Holstein (93 % ± 4 % overall accuracy, blind 100-point check). Needs a separate Python environment with Ultralytics (pip install ultralytics); everything runs locally. Developed with AI assistance. Licence AGPL-3.0 (Ultralytics).
